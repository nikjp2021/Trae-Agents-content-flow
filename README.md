# Articles-TRAE: The Automated AI Content Pipeline 🚀

**Scaling High-Quality Business Narratives with Multi-Agent Orchestration.**

Welcome to **Articles-TRAE**, a fully autonomous, multi-agent pipeline designed to research, write, enrich, and review high-quality business articles across LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads, and Instagram.

## 💡 The Problem & The Solution

Scalable content creation often suffers from a "quantity over quality" trap. Manual drafting is slow, while basic AI generation often lacks depth, accurate citations, or platform-native "soul."

**Articles-TRAE** solves this by deploying a specialized team of AI agents that don't just "write"—they **engineer** content. From deep-web research to 13-criteria quality audits, this pipeline ensures every post is data-backed, platform-optimized, and narrative-driven.

---

## �️ Human-in-the-Loop: The Integrity Standard

While the pipeline is fully automated, it is built on a **Trust but Verify** philosophy. The flow terminates at the "Finished" state, providing a comprehensive review file and audit trail. 

**Human review is the final gate.** This ensures:
- **No Fake Data**: Verification of all agent-sourced citations.
- **Narrative Alignment**: Guarding against wrong or misleading narratives.
- **Brand Integrity**: Final human polish before hitting "Publish."

---

## 🚀 The 5-Step Agentic Pipeline

1.  **Research** (`article-researcher`): Deep-web analysis and trend discovery.
2.  **Drafting** (`article-drafter`): Crafting distinct dialects for 4 platforms.
3.  **Organization** (`article-organizer`): Contextual sorting and structure management.
4.  **Enrichment** (`article-enricher`): Visual hooks, emojis, and AI image prompts.
5.  **Review** (`article-reviewer`): A rigorous **13-criteria weighted scoring** system (5.0 scale).

---

## � The "Secret Sauce": 13-Criteria Quality Control

Every article is audited against a weighted rubric to ensure excellence:
- **Core (Weighted 2x)**: Hook Strength, Citation Quality, Virality Potential.
- **Precision (Weighted 1x)**: Platform Fit, Tone Differentiation, Emoji Strategy, Structure & Flow, Length, Visual Formatting, **Context Grounding**, **Title Format**, **Alt Text Awareness**, and **No Generic Filler**.
