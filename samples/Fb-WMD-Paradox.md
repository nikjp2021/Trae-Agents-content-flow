# They Told Us Iraq Had Nukes. They Were Wrong. 20 Years Later, We're Still Paying.

🔥 Remember March 2003?

I do. I remember watching Colin Powell at the UN, holding up that vial of anthrax, telling the world that Iraq had weapons of mass destruction. I remember the flags, the speeches, the "shock and awe" on TV.

And I remember the silence when we found out there was nothing there.

Not a single stockpile. No active program. The UN inspectors said it. The CIA said it. The 1,400-person Iraq Survey Group spent years searching and came up empty.

**$1.1 trillion.** That's what the Iraq War cost in direct U.S. spending alone.

**4,400+ American lives.** Gone.

**187,000+ Iraqi civilians.** Dead.

All based on intelligence that the Senate Intelligence Committee later described as "unreliable sources" producing "false or exaggerated allegations."

💔 Here's what stings most: nobody was held accountable. The people who made the case for war wrote books, gave speeches, and in some cases returned to government. The rest of us? We paid the bill — in dollars, in lives, and in trust.

---

**The part nobody talks about:**

Before Saddam was our enemy, he was our ally. During the Iran-Iraq War in the 1980s, the U.S. provided intelligence to Iraq — fully aware, according to declassified CIA documents, that Saddam would use it to launch chemical weapons attacks. Mustard gas. Sarin. Against Iranian troops and Kurdish civilians.

The U.S. knew. And did it anyway.

Fast forward to 2003: the same man the West had helped arm with chemical weapons was now the reason we were going to war. The "existential threat" was, in part, one we had helped create.

🤔 It makes you wonder: how many "monsters" are we funding today that will become tomorrow's "existential threats"?

---

**The nuclear double standard is even worse:**

The Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT) was signed in 1968. It promised that nuclear-armed nations would eventually disarm, and in exchange, other nations wouldn't build nukes.

Here we are, 2026:

- The U.S. is spending **$1.7 trillion** to modernize its entire nuclear arsenal (Arms Control Association, May 2026)
- Russia and the U.S. still hold **~90% of all nuclear weapons** — about 12,241 warheads worldwide
- China is adding **100 warheads per year**
- Israel has an estimated **90 nukes** — and the U.S. has a secret policy forbidding officials from even acknowledging it

We tell Iran: "You can't have nukes."
We tell North Korea: "You can't have nukes."
We tell the world: "Trust us."

**But why should they?**

---

**The deeper pattern:**

Every generation, we identify an "evil ideology" — Nazism, communism, terrorism — and we build an enormous apparatus to fight it. Wars are fought. Budgets explode. Civil liberties shrink.

And when that enemy fades? We find a new one.

The apparatus doesn't shrink. It grows.

Nazi Germany fell in 1945. The Soviet Union collapsed in 1991. Al-Qaeda is a shadow. But the military budget? The surveillance state? The permanent state of war? They're bigger than ever.

Global military spending hit **$2.89 trillion in 2025** (SIPRI). The U.S. alone accounts for **$954 billion** of that — more than the next nine countries combined.

Who are we still fighting?

More importantly: **who protects us from the protectors?**
